Engineering Lead at Afila · Founder of Thuuba

I build software that solves real problems, and run the marketing that gets it found.

By day I’m Engineering Lead at Afila, leading the team behind platforms like E-SAKIP, E-Monev and Dikerja: the systems local governments in Indonesia use to plan, measure and report their work. The rest of my work goes wider: a bill-payment platform, a game top-up store, a coffee chain’s website, a daily dhikr app.

I also founded Thuuba, a digital marketing agency. There I run performance marketing on Meta Ads, Google Ads and TikTok Ads, with e-commerce alongside it. It keeps me honest as an engineer: I see what makes people stop, click and buy, then take that back into what I build.
