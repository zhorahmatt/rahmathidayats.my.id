AI and agents now run through both. I build agents and agent workflows that take on real engineering and marketing work, with Claude, ChatGPT and Grok.

About Rahmat Hidayat

Rahmat Hidayat is a software engineer and digital marketer based in Makassar, Indonesia. He is Engineering Lead at Afila, where he leads the team behind E-SAKIP, E-Monev and Dikerja, platforms that local governments in Indonesia use to plan, measure and report their work. He is also the founder of Thuuba, a digital marketing agency that runs performance marketing on Meta Ads, Google Ads and TikTok Ads. He builds AI agents and agent workflows for engineering and marketing work. His projects range from government platforms to a bill-payment platform, a game top-up store and a daily dhikr app, and he works with Laravel, Vue.js, React, Next.js, TypeScript, Go and PostgreSQL.
