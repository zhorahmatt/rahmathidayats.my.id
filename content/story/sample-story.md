This is a placeholder for the Stories section. A story works exactly like a blog post: one Markdown file in `content/story/`, with a title, a date and a one-sentence description at the top.

## What belongs here

That is yours to decide. The Blog is a natural place for how-to notes and opinions. Stories could be the longer pieces: how a project started, what went wrong on the way, what you would do differently.

## Replacing this

Delete `content/story/sample-story.md`, add your own file next to it, and push. The site rebuilds itself and the new story appears here and on the home page.
